Welcome to the Understanding-Operating-Systems series.

This series of notes will provide insight to the mechanisms to how running program code cooperate and utilize limited computing resources - an **operating system**.

Operating systems ultimately deal with the question:
> "Given a CPU, memory, and IO devices, how can I satisfy and complete the work given by a user and the programs they have?"


Grab your rubber chicken politicians, your task windows manager, and let's explore.
