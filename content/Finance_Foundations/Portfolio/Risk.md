- The **chance** that an investment's actual gain differs from the expected outcome.^risk-def
- The variability of [[Return|return]] around [[Expected Return|expected return]]
- It is measured by the [[Standard Deviation]] of an asset - based on their history. 
- Standard deviation measures the **volatility** of an investment relative to their historical averages. 
	- Higher volatility, higher potential difference in expected return, higher risk.
	- Lower volatility, more certainty in expected return, lower risk.

# Types of risk

![[Pasted image 20260223051526.png|500]]
- **Market Risk** - Risk that affects the entire economy (e.g., inflation, war, pandemics). 
	- You **cannot** get rid of this by diversifying.
	- Investors are typically compensated for this type.
	- All companies, all-economy wide.
- **Firm-specific Risk** - Risk unique to a single company or industry (e.g., a strike at PAL or a bad product launch at Apple). 
	- This **can** be eliminated through holding many stocks - aka [[Diversification]].

Since every firm is affected by the market risk, the measure for this is captured by [[Beta Sensitivity]]
