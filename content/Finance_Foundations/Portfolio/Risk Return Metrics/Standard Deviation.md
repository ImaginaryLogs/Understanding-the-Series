The square root of the variance; a measure of the "spread" or volatility of [[Return]].
$$
\sigma = \sqrt{\sigma^2}
$$
- Higher $\sigma$ → more volatile → more risky
- Lower $\sigma$ → more stable → less risky

# Variance
$$
\sigma^2=E[(r-\bar{r})^2]
$$
The distance of a given data point from the mean.
