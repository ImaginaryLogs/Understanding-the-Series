They are the quantitative tools used in **Modern Portfolio Theory (MPT)** to describe a probability distribution. In finance, it is not looked at a single price, but rather a cloud of possibilities. 

- Measures of Central Tendency
	- [[Arithmetic Average]] - measures next period's probability
		- "What is the cost of capital?"
	- [[Geometric Average]] - measures historical wealth growth
		- "Did I actually get richer?"
- Measures of Dispersion
	- [[Variance]] - squared offset away from mean
	- [[Standard Deviation]] - square root of variance
		- "How much could I lose?"
	- [[Coefficient of Variation]] - measures risk per unit of return
- Measures of Expectation
	- [[Expected Return]] - sum all possible outcomes weight by their probability
		- "What is the return from a weighted goal?"
	- [[Probability Distribution]]
