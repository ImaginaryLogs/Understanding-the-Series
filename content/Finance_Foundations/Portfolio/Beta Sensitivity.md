Measures **how sensitivity** a portfolio is to market movements.

$$
\beta_i = \frac{Cov(r_i,r_m)}{\sigma^2_m}
$$

## Interpretation

| Beta  | Meaning                   |
| ----- | ------------------------- |
| β > 1 | More volatile than market |
| β = 1 | Moves with market         |
| β < 1 | Less volatile             |
| β = 0 | No relation               |
| β < 0 | Moves opposite            |

>[!example]
> If amazon stocks is calculated to have $\beta= 1.47$, then for every $1\%$ the market moves, the stocks move $1.47\%$.
