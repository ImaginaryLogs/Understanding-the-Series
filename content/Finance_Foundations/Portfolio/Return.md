**Return.** Refers to the *gains and benefits received from an investment*. 
- **Think**: it is the percentage change in value of an investment over a period.
$$
r =\frac{\text{Ending Value}}{\text{Beginning Value}} - 1
$$

Use a **percentage** because it allows:
- Direct comparison between different investments regardless of their price. 
- Captures both capital gains (price increases) and income like dividends.

# Types of Return
**Nominal Return** - raw percentage return.
**Real Return** - return adjusted for inflation.

$$
{\text{Real Rate}} = \frac{1 + \text{Nominal Rate}}{1 + \text{Inflation Rate}} - 1
$$

You can measure the [[Expected Return]] of an investment to the stocks of a company.
