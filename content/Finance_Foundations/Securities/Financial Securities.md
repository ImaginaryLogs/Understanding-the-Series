Also known as **financial instruments** or [[Assets|Financial Assets]].

These are financial products that represent the right to receive future benefits under specific conditions

These are broadly classified as two of the following:
1. [[Equity Securities]] - benefits from ownership
	1. [[Stocks]]
2. [[Debt Securities]] - benefits from money borrowed
	1. [[Treasury Bills]]
	2. [[Treasury Bonds]]
	3. [[Money Market Securities]]
