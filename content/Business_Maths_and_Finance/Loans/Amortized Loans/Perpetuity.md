Perpetuity are a type of [[Loans and Interests|loan]] such that it is paid in *equal amounts* of cash or value; timed in *equidistant intervals* (monthly, quarterly, annually); and *eternally done* with no set date.^perpetuity-def

**Characteristics**:
- Equal Payment
- Equally Timed
- Eternal Contract

>[!examples]
>Some examples are from owning a business such as *dividend streams* and *preferred stocks*.

# Level Perpetuity
Here, the payments remain at a constant rate from period to period.

$$
\text{PV}=\frac{PMT}{i}
$$


# Growing Perpetuity
Here, the payments grows at a certain rate from period to period.

$$
\text{PV}=\frac{PMT_\text{period 1}}{i - g}
$$

Even just a small growth rate can have massive effects of value over time.
