Perpetuity are a type of [[Loans and Interests|loan]] such that it is paid in _equal amounts_ of cash or value; timed in _equidistant intervals_ (monthly, quarterly, annually); and _eternally done_ with no set date.^perpetuity-def

**Characteristics**:

- Equal Payment
- Equally Timed
- Eternal Contract

> [!examples]
> Some examples are from owning a business such as _dividend streams_ and _preferred stocks_.

# Level Perpetuity

Here, the payments remain at a constant rate from period to period.

$$
\text{PV}=\frac{PMT}{i}
$$

# Growing Perpetuity

Here, the payments grows at a certain rate from period to period.

$$
\text{PV}=\frac{PMT_\text{period 1}}{i - g}
$$

Even just a small growth rate can have massive effects of value over time.
