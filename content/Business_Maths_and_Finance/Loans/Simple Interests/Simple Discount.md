Revolves on the linear **deduction** of money from loan on **future** to a **present value.**^deduction-def

The Deal from the Creditor: "You want a $1,100 loan for a future business? I’ll take my $100 deduction fee right now. Here is $1,000. In one year, you owe me the full $1,100."

If a borrower collects **cash in advance** on an agreed upon simple interest loan, then it is called a simple discount.^simple-discount-def


>
> $$I_d=Fdt$$
>
> $$P_r=F-I_d$$
>


If I'm owed $P_r$ in the future, what is it worth to me right now?

| Term  | Name                           | Purpose                                          | Unit                | Example      |
| ----- | ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------- | ------------ |
| F     | Maturity value or Future Value | Amount paid at the end of term                   | Currenct            | $4,000       |
| $d$   | Discount                       | The shrink and adjusted amount from future value | Percentage per Year | 10% per year |
| $t$   | Term                           | Length of the term                               | Year                | 2 years      |
| $P_r$ | Proceeds of Loan               | The remaining value present in the loan          | Currency            | $2000        |
|       |                                |                                                  |                     |              |
