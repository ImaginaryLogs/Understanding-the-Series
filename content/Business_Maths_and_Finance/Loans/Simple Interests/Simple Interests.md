Revolves on the linear **accumulation** of money from loan on **present value** to a **future value**.^accumulation-def

The Deal from the Creditor: "I will give you $1,000 today. In one year, you give me that $1,000 back plus $100 interest."

Here, the purpose is to ask: what is the **added** charge when borrowing?^simple-interest-def

>$$I=Prt$$

| Term | Name             | Purpose                                      | Unit              | Example     |
| ---- | ---------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ----------------- | ----------- |
| $P$  | Principle        | Amount borrowed at the start of the loan.    | Currency          | $40,000     |
| $r$  | Rate of Interest | Added amount of cash charged from borrowing. | Currency per Year | 6% per Year |
| $t$  | Term of the Loan | Bounded length of time                       | Years             | 2 Years     |
# Accrued Amount of Simple Interest
This shows the amount paid by the borrower at the end of a borrowed loan.^accrued-amount-simple-interest-def

>$$A=P+I=P(1+rt)$$

| Term | Name                             | Purpose                                          | Unit     | Example |
| ---- | -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | -------- | ------- |
| A    | Accrued Amount or Maturity Value | Amount paid at the end of term                   |          |         |
| $P$  | Principle                        | The added amount of cash charged from borrowing. | Currency | $40,000 |
| I    | Interest (derived)               | **Added** charge when borrowing                  | Currency | $8,000  |
