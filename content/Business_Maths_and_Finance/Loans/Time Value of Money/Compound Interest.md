Here, it predicts **future value** of an compounding investment over years.^compounding-def

> $$\text{FV}=\text{PV}(1 + i_{\text{rate}})^{n_{\text{years}}}$$

| Term               | Name             | Purpose                                                      | Unit                  | Example      |
| ------------------ | ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------- | ------------ |
| $\text{FV}$        | Future Value     | Predicted value of a compounding investment across some time | Currency              | $1464        |
| $\text{PV}$        | Present Value    | Initial value to place in an investment                      | Currency              | $1000        |
| $i_{\text{rate}}$  | Compounding Rate | Earning gained from an investment                            | Percentage over years | 10% per Year |
| $n_{\text{years}}$ | Term             | Length of compound interest                                  | Years                 | 4            |
The best way to view this is with a [[Timeline Model]], this represents the positive growth of income of an investment.
# Compound Interests with Frequent Terms
Predicts the **future value** of compound interest with high frequency periods.

> $$\text{FV}_{\text{nth year}}=\text{PV}(1 + \frac{i_{\text{annual}}}{\text{m}_{\text{periods}}})^{\text{m}_{\text{periods}}\times n_{\text{years}}}$$

Banks may offer days, months, or quarter frequency periods, so they have higher **compounding frequency**.^compounding-frequency-def

| Term               | Name             | Purpose                                                      | Unit                  | Example           |
| ------------------ | ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------- | ----------------- |
| $\text{FV}$        | Future Value     | Predicted value of a compounding investment across some time | Currency              | $1464             |
| $\text{PV}$        | Present Value    | Iinitial value to place in an investment                     | Currency              | $1000             |
| $i_{\text{rate}}$  | Compounding Rate | Earning gained from an investment                            | Percentage over years | 10% per Year      |
| $n_{\text{years}}$ | Term             | Length of compound interest                                  | Years                 | 4                 |
| $m_\text{periods}$ | Compounding Term | Terms present in Year                                        | Term per Year         | 360 Days per Year |
